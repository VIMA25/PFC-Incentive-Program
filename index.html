The Patient
Incentive Card

A premium card worth $5,000 toward a full IVF cycle — earned by our patients, never expiring, and theirs to keep for a second cycle or to pass to someone beginning the journey.

1

How it works

1

Earned

Given to a patient who completes an IVF cycle — a thank-you, not a coupon anyone can get.

2

Kept or given

She redeems it for her own next cycle, or hands it across the table to someone starting theirs.

3

Redeemed

Brought to any of our four Hampton Roads locations toward a full cycle. It never expires.

2

Policies & terms

Value
Up to $5,000 discount toward one full IVF cycle — a discount toward care, not cash or stored credit.
Eligibility
Issued to patients who complete an IVF cycle.
Expiration
Never expires.
Transfer
Freely transferable; in practice redeemed by a new patient beginning care.
Redemption
One full IVF cycle, any location. One per cycle. Not combinable with other offers.
Balance
Recipient covers the remaining balance; Affirm / PatientFi financing available.
Recognition
Givers are thanked when their card is redeemed — never paid for referring.
Tracking
Each card is individually numbered for attribution and recognition.

Incrementality break-even

A discounted self-pay cycle still nets ~$10.4k — comfortably above the ~$8k we keep on an insured cycle. The program creates value the moment more than ~1 in 3 redemptions (the ~32% break-even) come from patients who would not otherwise have come, and much of its cost simply formalizes the ~$360k/yr we already give away in ad-hoc discounts. *The 12-month figure understates true repeat behavior; a 24-month cohort read is planned.

4

Pilot & guardrails

A bounded test

Launch as a capped 100-card pilot with a randomized holdout group and a one-question intake at redemption — “would you have done this cycle without the card?” — to measure true incrementality. ~9-month read; the fixed card cap bounds exposure. A Virginia healthcare-law opinion on transferability is required before any card is issued.

What we expect to achieve
Measure the unknown
A real incrementality rate — the single number the whole case rests on — instead of an estimate.
Clear the break-even
Redemptions running above the ~32% incrementality bar, so the program is net-positive after cannibalization.
Lift loyalty & referrals
A higher return-cycle rate in the card group than the holdout, and referrals above the 17% word-of-mouth baseline.
A clear go / no-go
Pre-committed thresholds that say plainly whether to scale, redesign, or stop.
